✦a little about me

My path has been a little non-linear. I started at Broward College's Honors program, transferred to the University of Miami on a President's Scholarship, and spent time as a data analyst at a private bank and a brain-machine interface researcher before finding my way to the thing I love most: hardware.

These days I'm at UM's Robotics & Mechatronics Lab, where I co-lead the build of a Berkeley Humanoid Lite — a full open-source humanoid robot — as one of its founding undergraduate researchers. When I'm not in the lab, I'm probably at UMaker, the student makerspace I help run, or at my desk routing traces in KiCad.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships in embedded systems, firmware, and hardware engineering.
