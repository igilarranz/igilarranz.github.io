✦a little about me

I'm an EE + CS double major with a math minor and a focus in data science.

My path here was a little non-linear: I started in Broward College's Honors program, transferred to UM on a President's Scholarship, and along the way worked as a data analyst at a private bank and a brain-machine interface researcher. Somewhere between the dashboards and the EEG signals, I figured out that the part I loved most was the hardware underneath it all.

These days you'll find me at UM's Robotics & Mechatronics Lab, co-leading the build of a full open-source humanoid robot, at UMaker — the student makerspace I help run — or at my desk routing traces in KiCad.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 internships in embedded systems, firmware, and hardware engineering.
