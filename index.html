a little about me

I'm an Electrical Engineering and Computer Science student at the University of Miami, with a minor in Mathematics. I'm especially interested in embedded systems, robotics, and projects where hardware and software have to work together.

I'm currently doing research at UM's Robotics & Mechatronics Lab, where I work on autonomous systems and task planning for the BEHAVIOR Challenge 2026 and help build an open-source humanoid robot.

Outside the lab, I design my own boards and build embedded systems. I'm happiest on projects where the hardware and firmware are both mine to figure out.

things i've made

  • BEHAVIOR Challenge planner100 / 100 tasks

    The symbolic planning layer for our lab's entry. I wrote the compiler from BDDL task specs to PDDL, defined the predicates and operators, and connected it to Fast Downward. All 100 benchmark tasks produce a plan, with a CI check to keep it that way.

    BDDL → PDDL · Fast Downward · 62 operators · coverage analysis · CI

  • MotionSense-IMUbring-up

    Designed and fabricated a two-layer USB-C IMU board from schematic through assembly. The boards are back from JLCPCB, and I’m validating power rails, I²C communication, and sensor bring-up before writing the driver.

    LSM6DSM 6-axis IMU · AP2112K 3.3 V LDO · USB-C power · 2-layer
    project log →github ↗

  • Pomo-01released

    A pomodoro timer that looks like a tiny handheld. The canvas is 128×160, the same as a real ST7735 display, so it could run on one someday. Hand-drawn bitmap font, and a deer that runs while you work and sleeps when you stop.

    128×160 canvas · hand-set bitmap font · sprite animation · Electron build for Mac

  • Portable MP3 playerwip

    A music player built from modules: ESP32-S3, a small display, a DAC, microSD, a battery. So far the SD card reads, audio plays over I2S, and the display is running. Case and UI are next.

    ESP32-S3 · I2S DAC · ILI9341 display · microSD over SPI

  • Berkeley Humanoid Litein progress

    An open-source humanoid we're assembling at the lab. I joined halfway through and inherited two spreadsheets that disagreed about what had been built and bought, so I built one tracker that was actually true. That's how we found out the motors for 12 of the 22 actuators had never been ordered.

    22 actuators · MAD 5010 motors · master tracker: registry, phases, orders, issues

  • EEG pattern analysis2024

    Signal processing for EEG recordings from a behavioral study, from a summer in a brain-machine interface lab. Presented at the Pillars Conference.
